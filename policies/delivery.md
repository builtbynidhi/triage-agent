## delivery.estimates

Delivery takes 2 to 4 business days to metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune), 5 to 7 business days to other cities, and 7 to 10 business days to remote locations. Estimates count business days from dispatch and exclude Sundays and public holidays.

## delivery.dispatch

Orders placed before 2 pm IST on a business day are dispatched the same day; later orders are dispatched the next business day. Made-to-order items may take up to 5 additional business days, shown on the product page.

## delivery.delayed

If an order has not arrived 3 business days after the end of its estimated delivery window, support escalates it to a human agent to investigate with the courier. If the parcel is confirmed lost, the customer chooses a free replacement or a full refund. The agent does not promise either before the investigation completes.

## delivery.tracking

A tracking link is emailed and sent by SMS when the order is dispatched. Support can read the order status but does not have live courier scan data; for the exact location the customer should use the tracking link.
