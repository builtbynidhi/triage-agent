## returns.window

A customer may return an item within 7 days of the delivery date. The 7 days are counted from the day the order is marked delivered; the delivery day itself is day 0, so an item delivered on the 2nd can be returned up to and including the 9th. A return requested on day 8 or later is outside the window and cannot be approved as a standard return.

## returns.condition

The item must be unused, undamaged and in its original packaging with all tags, accessories and manuals. Sealed items are always accepted inside the window. Opened items are accepted only if they are unused and complete. Items showing signs of use or wear are declined.

## returns.exclusions

The following cannot be returned: innerwear and personal hygiene products once opened, perishable goods, customised or personalised items, gift cards, and items marked "final sale" on the product page. A defect or damage on an excluded item is handled under the damaged goods policy, not as a return.

## returns.process

The customer starts a return by contacting support with the order ID. We arrange a free pickup from the delivery address within 2 to 3 business days. The customer hands the item over in its original packaging. After the pickup is quality-checked, the refund is processed as described in the refunds policy. The customer does not need to ship anything themselves.
