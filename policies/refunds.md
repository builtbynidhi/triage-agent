## refunds.window

A refund can be requested within 14 days of the delivery date, counted with the delivery day as day 0. A refund for a returned item is issued only after the return was accepted under the returns policy, so the item must also be inside the 7-day return window. After 14 days no refund is issued; the customer may be offered store credit at the discretion of a human agent.

## refunds.method

Refunds go back to the original payment method. For cash-on-delivery orders the customer must provide a bank account or UPI ID; support collects it and a human agent confirms before payment. We never refund to a different card or to a third party.

## refunds.timeline

Once the returned item passes quality check, the refund is processed within 5 to 7 business days. Bank and card networks may take a further 2 to 3 business days to show it. Support must not state that a refund is approved or paid until it has been processed; an agent may only describe the process and timeline.

## refunds.exclusions

Shipping and cash-on-delivery handling fees are not refunded unless the return was caused by our error. Gift wrapping fees are not refunded. Promotional discounts are deducted proportionally if only part of an order is returned.

## refunds.high_value

Any refund of 10000 INR or more needs manager approval and must be escalated to a human agent. The agent must not promise the amount or the outcome to the customer.

## refunds.damaged

If an item arrived damaged, defective or different from what was ordered, the refund or replacement is decided by a human agent under the damaged goods policy. This applies even when the customer is outside the standard return window, as long as the issue was reported in time. The agent must not approve or promise such a refund itself.
