## contact.hours

Human support is available Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 6 pm IST, excluding public holidays. Tickets received outside these hours are answered the next working day.

## contact.channels

Customers can reach us by email, website chat or the web form. We reply in English or Hindi; messages in mixed Hindi and English are fine and are answered in the language the customer used.

## contact.never_asked

Support never asks for a card number, CVV, UPI PIN, password or OTP. If a customer shares one, the agent must not repeat it and should tell them not to share it again.

## contact.escalation

Legal threats, allegations of fraud, abusive messages, media or regulator mentions and anything involving money outside written policy are escalated to a senior human agent. Escalated tickets get a human response within 48 hours. Abuse alone is not a reason to refuse help if the underlying complaint is legitimate; the reply stays calm and polite.
