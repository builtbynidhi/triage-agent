## cancellation.before_dispatch

An order can be cancelled free of charge at any time before it is dispatched. The customer asks support with the order ID. An order whose status is "placed" has not been dispatched and can be cancelled.

## cancellation.after_dispatch

Once an order is dispatched it cannot be cancelled. The customer can refuse delivery at the door, or accept it and then return it under the returns policy if it is inside the return window.

## cancellation.refund

For a prepaid order cancelled before dispatch, the full amount is refunded to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days. Cash-on-delivery orders have nothing to refund. We may also cancel an order ourselves if an item is out of stock, in which case the customer is told by email.
