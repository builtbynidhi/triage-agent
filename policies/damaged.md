## damaged.reporting

Damage, defects or a wrong item must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. The customer should send the order ID and at least one clear photo of the item and the outer packaging. Reports after 48 hours are still escalated to a human agent, who decides case by case.

## damaged.resolution

For a verified damaged, defective or wrong item the customer can choose a free replacement or a full refund, including shipping. The item is picked up free of charge. Every damaged goods case is verified by a human agent; the support agent only collects details and escalates, and never confirms the resolution itself.

## damaged.safety

If the item is a safety risk, for example it overheats, smokes, sparks, leaks or has broken glass, tell the customer to stop using it immediately and keep it away from children. Escalate at once with high priority. Do not suggest repairs or workarounds.
