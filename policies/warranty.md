## warranty.period

Electronics and appliances carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. Accessories such as cables, covers and chargers carry 6 months. Warranty is separate from the return window: a product that stops working after the 7-day return window may still be covered here.

## warranty.exclusions

Warranty does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, damage from misuse, repairs by unauthorised persons, or normal wear such as battery capacity decline or cosmetic scratches.

## warranty.claims

To claim, the customer sends the order ID and describes the problem, with a photo or video if possible. A human agent creates a service request with the manufacturer's service partner. The support agent never confirms that a claim is accepted; it escalates with the details collected.
